What I did

From raw data to business insight

Clean

02

Analyse

Used pandas groupby, aggregation and sorting to examine revenue, products, categories, regions, customers, salespeople and discounts.

Exported cleaned data to Excel and used KPI cards, pivot tables, charts and slicers to make the results easier to understand.

Business insights

What the data says

Discounts

The 20–40% discount band generated the most total revenue in this dataset, while the 40–60% band generated less. Larger discounts therefore do not automatically mean more revenue.
